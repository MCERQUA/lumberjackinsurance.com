## What they do well
- forestrymutual + hummel lean on niche credibility (logging-specific programs).
- Most list: logging truck, inland marine equipment, loggers WC, forestry GL.
- Trust signals: AM Best ratings, program underwriters, state filings (ICC/MC).

## Gaps we exploit
- **Design/UX:** nearly all are dated, text-heavy, low-conversion. We ship a
  modern light design with a clear quote funnel + sticky phone.
- **Specificity:** we spell out the *exact* equipment (feller bunchers, forwarders,
  skidders, delimbers, knuckle-boom loaders) and the *exact* coverages
  (loggers broad form, LCP endorsement, unloading liability) — most competitors stay generic.
- **Conversion:** multi-step quote form + 15-min turnaround SLA + 50-state licensing.
- **Content:** cost-guide blog (the highest-intent informational cluster they under-serve).

## Positioning
"Lumberjack Insurance, by Contractors Choice Agency — specialty coverage for
logging & forestry operations. Licensed all 50 states. 20+ years. Former-contractor
credibility. 15-minute quotes."
